A LETTER TO MY JAANU

I am truly
sorry.

My love,

I want you to know something from the bottom of my heart. I have never wanted to make you feel disrespected, unwanted, or less important. If anything I said or did made you feel that way, I am genuinely sorry.

I have always considered you someone incredibly precious in my life. I have considered you my Ghar Ki Laxmi, someone I want to respect, cherish, protect and make happy. You deserve to feel loved and valued, not just through my words but through everything I do.

I know I am not perfect. I make mistakes, I have things to learn, and there are parts of myself that I need to improve. But please believe me when I say that I am trying to become a better person, not just for myself, but for you and for the relationship we share.

I don't want to be someone who simply promises to change whenever things go wrong. I want to actually work on myself and let my actions show you that I mean every word.

You are important to me in ways I cannot fully explain. I never want you to question your worth or feel that I don't see how special you are.

I am sorry, meri jaan.
Truly, from the deepest part of my heart.

Yours Bitti, with all my love. ♥
